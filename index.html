Methodology & data notes

Efficiency Score = 70% × tradedLiquidityShare + 30% × (tradedBins ÷ fundedBins). Idle capital = TVL × (1 − tradedLiquidityShare). "Fee fair" = protocol + curator spread fee ≤ 40%. Metric's public API returns bin counts per window, not a per-bin trade log, so the heatmap shows real per-bin liquidity from live depth and marks the bins nearest the current price as active in proportion to tradedBins/fundedBins (an estimate). Opportunity cost = idle capital × max(0, markout APR of traded bins). Uniform-bin APR = markout APR × tradedLiquidityShare. Trend snapshots are stored only in your browser. APR of a window where only a small bin traded can be extreme, so opportunity cost and the simulator are hidden when traded share is under 2%. Not financial advice.
